🔒 Privacy

Hand Cricket is designed to work without an account.

Data: The game stores local game progress, preferences, achievements and statistics in your browser when storage is available.

No email required: Sign-in only needs a username and an optional password, stored on your own device to keep your profile separate from other players on the same browser. No email addresses or external accounts are collected.

Platform services: When played on a supported game portal, that portal's SDK may provide advertising, account, cloud-save or analytics services according to its own privacy policy.

Multiplayer: This release supports local pass-and-play and an online friend mode. Online networking is provided through the supported portal networking service.
